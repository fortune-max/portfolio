Leo's style: bold editorial type, one big statement per product

What I took from leo-fezard.dev: huge display type, a small monospace companion font, one accent colour, alternating dark and light sections, a strong statement for each project and the tech stack as pills. Below are three takes that are clearly yours, not a copy of Leo's. The copy is A's voice, rewritten for headlines.

The closest to Leo's energy, with a different palette and voice. Each product gets a "before → after" headline with a strikethrough. Green suggests "systems are live and healthy".
